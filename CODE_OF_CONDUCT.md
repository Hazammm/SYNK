# Code of conduct

Synk is built by a small team and is open to contributors. We want everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, reviews or discussions, to feel welcome and safe.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and kind, including when you disagree.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and accept feedback the same way.
- Use language that includes people of every background, identity and level of experience.
- Respect people's time and privacy. Do not share anyone's personal information without their permission.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, or deliberately disrupting discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address, phone number or email, without their consent.

## Where this applies

This code applies in every project space: the repository, issues, pull requests, reviews, and any chat or meeting about the project. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If something happens that breaks this code, contact the maintainers privately through the email address on their GitHub profiles. Please include links or screenshots if you can. Reports are kept confidential, and the person reporting will not face any retaliation.

For security problems, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## What happens next

The maintainers review every report and respond as soon as they can. Depending on what happened, they may:

1. **Remind**: a private note explaining what was wrong.
2. **Warn**: a formal warning, with clear consequences if it happens again.
3. **Ban temporarily**: no interaction with the project for a set time.
4. **Ban permanently**: for serious or repeated violations.

This code is adapted from the ideas in the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
